# The NeoBankers Group

**The NeoBankers Group Limited** is a United Kingdom financial‑technology holding company, incorporated in England and Wales (Company No. 13821747) and based in London. It is the intellectual‑property and trade‑mark holding company of the Group.

## What we do

The Group designs and owns the architecture of the **Integrated Digital Financial Ecosystem (IDFE)** — a single, ledger‑agnostic platform with **FinOS** as its operating system and **FinKernel** as its execution kernel, built as an IT solution for licensed institutions across payments and digital assets.

The Group designs and owns the architecture. It does **not** itself provide payment, e‑money, banking or investment services; those are provided by the licensed institutions that use the platform.

## Group structure

- **The NeoBankers Group Limited** — England and Wales, Company No. 13821747. Intellectual‑property and trade‑mark holder.
